Since 2021, Anthropy has provided an outreach platform for students to foster early intellectual curiosity and
meaningful engagement within the humanities and social sciences. It is with great pride that we present the second
edition of the Anthropy Review, a journal celebrating the outstanding work of our student researchers. We are equally
delighted to have launched In2Academia, our flagship research programme, this year — a structured curriculum
designed by published researchers, paired with one-on-one mentorship from expert mentors.

This issue presents a diverse range of research, each paper offering a fresh perspective on complex topics related to
identity, culture, and power. Ava Tso's "How does Confucianist philosophy influence family-based mental health
interventions in Singapore?" examines how Confucian values of filial piety and social harmony shape mental health
support amid stigma and cultural expectation. Ashita Agarwal's "From Syncretism to Majoritarianism: The Evolution of
Religious Tolerance from Akbar's Rule to BJP-Led India" traces the shift from Akbar's inclusive policies to the BJP's
identity-driven politics. Vaishnav Krishnaprabhu's "Colonial Monopolies and Industrial Decline" investigates how the
British East India Company's trade policies stunted Indian industrialisation between 1608 and 1874. Samarth Bharat
Puri's "Indian Army's Role in the Integration of the Princely States Between 1947 and 1950" highlights the military's
role in India's post-independence political consolidation. Mysha Shangari's "Gods, Mortals and Misogyny" analyses how
divine archetypes in Greek mythology shaped early narratives of gender and power. And Farhad Driver's "Prevalent
Differences Between Iranian Zoroastrians and Indian Parsees" compares how the two communities diverged culturally,
economically, and socially after the Arab conquest of Persia.

This journal represents a three-month process of dedication and collaboration, with ten sessions devoted to refining
each paper through feedback and discussion. We thank all the mentors who devoted their energy and time to guiding
the students throughout this process — their support has been invaluable in shaping these papers and ensuring the
quality of this journal.
