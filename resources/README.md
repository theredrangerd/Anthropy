# Resources

Source material for site content, kept outside `src/` since none of it is
consumed directly by the build. Anything the site actually serves is copied
into `public/` (optimised) first.

## Links

- **Wix site (previous/parallel site, source of historical content):**
  https://anthropy.wixsite.com/website

## Folders

- `journals/` — journal PDFs: `anthropy-2026-journal.pdf` (The Anthropy
  Review, 3rd edition; its 5 papers were extracted into
  `src/content/articles/`) plus the 2023–24 and 2024–25 In2Academia journals.
- `leadership team/` — member photos (`Noah-Austin-pic.png`, `anahita pic.jpg`,
  `vaish pic.jpg`). Published, cropped versions live in `public/leadership/`.
- `brand-resources/` — logo/emblem screenshots and WhatsApp-exported event
  photos (`IMG-20260914-WA00xx.jpg`), undated as delivered; check with the team
  before attaching them to specific timeline events. The Anthropy globe emblem
  used on the About page is `Screenshot 2026-10-06 …png`.
- `HFW/` — Humanities Focus Week photos. Some are published as
  `public/events/hfw-*.jpg` and `public/about/forum.jpg`.
- `session-images/` — In2Academia workshop photos (HEIC) and a reference
  image. One is published as `public/about/workshop.jpg`.

The photo folders (`brand-resources/`, `HFW/`, `session-images/`) contain
unreleased photos of students and are kept local, not committed to git.
